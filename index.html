ABOUT THIS COLLECTION

An honest record of the work.

This portfolio documents four projects completed for UNC Charlotte’s ITCS 3162 course. The case studies clarify the original notebooks, preserve their visual results, and call out small samples, modeling assumptions, and data gaps. The notebooks are included with saved outputs removed; the underlying CSV datasets are not redistributed here.
