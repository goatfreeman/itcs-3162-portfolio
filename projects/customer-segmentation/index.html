Exploring customer segments

THE QUESTION

What I wanted to understand

Can purchasing and engagement patterns be grouped into a small number of customer segments?

THE WORKFLOW

From raw data to a testable view

The notebook removes Education and Marital_Status, standardizes numeric columns, uses PCA and an elbow/silhouette review to explore cluster counts, then fits K-means with three clusters.

WHAT THE RUN SHOWED

Findings, with caveats.

Observed finding

After rows with missing values are dropped, 2,216 records remain. The selected three-cluster run contains groups of 1,040, 635, and 541 records. Correlation and cluster-profile plots help inspect patterns; the notebook does not establish a validated customer strategy.

What to keep in mind

The analysis omits two categorical fields, includes ID among standardized numeric features, and drops 24 rows with missing values. Those choices can affect distance-based clusters. The three groups need further validation and substantive interpretation before being used for business decisions.

Next iteration: I would exclude identifier columns, encode categorical variables deliberately, compare cluster stability across seeds, and validate any proposed segment against outcomes not used to create it.
